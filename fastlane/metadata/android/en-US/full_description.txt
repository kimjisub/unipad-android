UniPad is a rhythm game played on a Launchpad-style pad grid. Each song is a UniPack: a pack of sounds, LED lights and, in many packs, an autoplay track.

Get packs
- Download packs from the Store inside the app (more than 70 packs; needs an internet connection).
- Import a UniPack .zip file you already have (Import downloaded packs).
- UniPacks are made outside the app by the community. UniPad plays them; it does not create them.

Practice
- Autoplay plays the whole song with its lights.
- Guide plays the song and marks the pads to press.
- Step waits until you press the marked pads.
Practice modes work with packs that include an autoplay track.

Play your way
- No Launchpad? Just tap the pads on screen.
- Connect a Launchpad or another MIDI controller with a USB cable. Your phone may need an adapter for its port. Common Launchpad models are detected automatically; otherwise choose your model in the app. We have not tested every phone, cable and controller.
- Change the look with themes.
- Once a pack is on your phone, you can play it without an internet connection.
